A [[Differential Manifold|differential manifold]] $M$ equipped with a [[Riemannian Metric|Riemannian metric]] $g:\mathfrak{X}(M)\times\mathfrak{X}(M)\to \mathbb{R}$ is called a **Riemannian manifold**. And $M$ equipped with a [[Riemannian Manifold|pseudo-Riemannian metric]] is called a **pseudo-Riemannian manifold**.
