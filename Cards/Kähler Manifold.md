Let $(M,J,g)$ be a [[Hermitian Manifold|Hermitian manifold]]. The **Kähler 2-form** $\omega$ is defined by
$$\omega(X,Y)=g(J(X),Y),\quad X,Y\in \mathfrak{X}(M).$$
A **Kähler manifold** is $M$ with a [[Exterior Differential Form#Related|closed]] Kähler 2-form, i.e. $\mathrm{d}\omega=0$.

>[!note] Equivalent definition
>Existence of closed Kähler 2-form is equivalent to $\nabla J=0$, where $\nabla$ is a [[Levi-Civita Connection|Levi-Civita connection]].
