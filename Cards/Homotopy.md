Let $X,Y$ be [[Topological Space|topological spaces]]. For $f,g:X\to Y$ two [[Continuous Map (Topology)|continuous maps]], the **homotopy** of $f,g$ is a continuous map
$$\eta:X\times [0,1]\to Y$$
s.t.
$$\eta(x,0)=f(x),\eta(x,1)=g(x).$$

Homotopy is an [[Equivalence Relation|equivalence relation]], the according [[Equivalence Class|equivalence class]] is called **homotopy class**.
