**Natural units** is a measurement system for which selected [[Z Physical Constants|physical constants]] have been set to 1. Natural units simplify the form of many equations, by removing multiple constants.

Now set the [[Z Speed of Light|speed of light]] $c$, [[Planck Constant|reduced Planck constant]] $\hbar$, [[Z Boltzmann Constant|Boltzmann constant]] $k_{\mathrm{B}}$ to 1. The [[Z Dimension of Units|dimensions of units]] are now
$$[\text{length}]=[\text{time}]=[\text{energy}]^{-1}=[\text{temperature}]^{-1}.$$
Usually one choose to use energy as the basic unit.
