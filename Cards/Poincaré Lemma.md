The **Poincaré lemma** states that, if a [[Neighborhood|neighborhood]] $U$ of a [[Differential Manifold|manifold]] $M$ is [[Contractible Space|contractible]] to a point $p_{0}\in M$, any [[Exterior Differential Form#Related|closed r-form]] on $U$ is also [[Exterior Differential Form#Related|exact]].
