Let $S$ be a [[Set|set]] defined with a [[Equivalence Relation|equivalence relation]] $\sim$. The set of all [[Equivalence Class|equivalence classes]] is called the **quotient set**
$$S /\sim = \{ [x]:x\in S \}.$$There is a [[Map|map]] called **quotient projection**
$$\pi: \begin{matrix}
S & \to & S /\sim \\ x&\mapsto&[x]
\end{matrix}.$$
