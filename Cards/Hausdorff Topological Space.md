A [[Topological Space|topological space]] $(X,\tau)$ is a **Hausdorff space** if, for an arbitrary pair of distinct points $x,x'\in X$, there always exists [[Topological Space#Related concepts|neighborhoods]] $U\ni x$ and $U'\ni x$ such that $U\cap U'=\varnothing$.
