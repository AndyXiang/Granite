Let $(X,\tau)$ be a [[Topological Space|topological space]] defined with an [[Equivalence Relation|equivalence relation]] $\sim$. Let $\pi:X\to X/\sim$ be the [[Quotient Set|projection]]. There is a natural topology $\rho$, called **quotient topology**, on $X /\sim$ as
$$\rho=\{ U\subset X /\sim: \pi ^{-1}(U)\in \tau \}.$$
We call $(X /\sim, \rho)$ the **quotient (topological) space**.
