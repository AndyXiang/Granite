Let $(X,\tau)$ be a [[Topological Space|topological space]]. A family $\{ A_{i}:i\in I,A_{i}\subset X \}$ is called a **covering** of $X$, if
$$\bigcup_{i\in I}A_{i}=X.$$
If all $A_{i}$ happen to be open sets in $\tau$, the covering is called an **open covering**.

A topological space $(X,\tau)$ is **compact** iff all of its open coverings $\{ U_{i} \}$ has a finite subset that is also a covering of $X$.

## Properties
1. Let $(X,\tau)$ be a [[Hausdorff Topological Space|Hausdorff topological space]]. Then its subset $A\subset X$ is compact iff it's [[Topological Space#Related concepts|closed]].
