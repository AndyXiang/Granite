Under the idea of [[Quantum Field Theory|QFT]], one is assumed to have a [[Lebesgue Measure|measure]] $\mu$ over the [[Projective Space|projective space]] of matter field $\phi$ and [[Gauge Field|gauge field]] $A$. In **path integral quantization**, observables are considered as [[Functional|functionals]] $\mathcal{O}[A,\phi]$. The expectation value of $\mathcal{O}[A,\phi]$ is then the *normalized integral weighted by [[Action (Physics)|action]]*:
$$\left\langle \mathcal{O} \right\rangle= \frac{\int \mu[\phi,A]\mathcal{O}[\phi,A]\mathrm{e}^{ \mathrm{i}S[\phi,A] }}{\int\mu[\phi,A] \mathrm{e}^{ \mathrm{i}S[\phi,A] }}. $$
