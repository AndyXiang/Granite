Let $K$ be a [[Simplex|simplicial complex]]. The $r$th **Betti number** $b_{r}(K)$ is
$$b_{r}(K)=\mathrm{dim}(H_{r}(K;\mathbb{R})).$$
Here $\mathbb{R}$ means we extends the coefficients of the [[Chain Complex|chain groups]] from $\mathbb{Z}$ to $\mathbb{R}$.
