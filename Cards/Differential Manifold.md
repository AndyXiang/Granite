Let $M$ be a [[Topological Manifold|topological manifold]]. A **differential structure** on $M$ is a set of [[Topological Manifold|charts]] $\{ (U,\phi):U\subset M,\phi\in[M,\mathbb{R}^{n}] \}$ that for any two charts $(U,\phi)$ and $(V,\psi)$, the mapping $\phi \circ \psi ^{-1}:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is differentiable. A manifold with differential structure is a **differential manifold**.

If the differential structure is $C^{\infty}$ differentiable, then we call it a **smooth manifold**.

## Related concepts
1. The **functions** on differential manifolds are [[Map|mappings]] $f:M\to \mathbb{R}$. If for every chart $(U,\phi)$, $\phi ^{-1}\circ f:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}$ is $C^{\infty}$ differentiable, then $f$ is a **smooth function**.
2. A **curve** on differential manifold $M$ is a mapping $c:\mathbb{R}\to M$.
