Let $\mathbf{A}$ be an [[Abelian Category|abelian category]], and $C_{\bullet}$ be a [[Chain Complex & Cochain complex|chain complex]]. The **$n$th homology [[Group|group]]** of $C_{\bullet}$ is
$$H_{n}(C_{\bullet})=\mathrm{ker}(d_{n}) /\mathrm{im}(d_{n-1}),n\in \mathbb{Z}.$$
This is also a [[Functor|functor]] $H_{n}:\mathbf{Ch}(\mathbf{A})\to \mathbf{A}$.

Let $C^{\bullet}$ be a [[Chain Complex & Cochain complex|cochain complex]]. The **$n$th cohomology [[Group|group]]** of $C^{\bullet}$ is
$$H^{n}(C^{\bullet})=\mathrm{ker}(d^{n}) /\mathrm{im}(d^{n-1}),n\in \mathbb{Z}.$$
This is also a [[Functor|functor]] $H^{n}:\mathbf{Coch}(\mathbf{A})\to \mathbf{A}$.
