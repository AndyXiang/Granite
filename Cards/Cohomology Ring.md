Let $M$ be a [[Differential Manifold|manifold]] and $H^{r}(M),r=1,2,\cdots,\mathrm{dim}(M)$ be its [[de Rham Cohomology Group|cohomology groups]]. The **Cohomology ring**
$$H^{*}(M)=\bigoplus _{r=1}^{\mathrm{dim}(M)} H^{r}(M),$$
where the multiplication is induced by [[Exterior Algebra|exterior product]] $\wedge:H^{*}(M)\times H^{*}(M)\to H^{*}(M)$.
