Consider a $d$-dimensional (pseudo-)[[Riemannian Manifold]] $(X,g)$ as the [[Spacetime|spacetime]]. For simplicity we do not consider $g$ as a dynamic object, thus no gravity. Denote the [[Isometry Group|isometry]] of spacetime as $G_{\mathrm{iso}}$ and a [[Gauge Theory|gauge symmetry]] as $G_{\mathrm{gauge}}$. The [[Symmetry|symmetry]] is now $G=G_{\mathrm{iso}}\times G_{\mathrm{gauge}}$. The [[Z Field Theory|field theory]] over the spacetime is composed of **matter field** $\phi$ as [[Fiber Bundle|section]] of the $G$-invariant [[Tangent Bundle|tangent bundle]] (spinor fields are slightly different), and [[Gauge Field|gauge field]] $A$ on the [[Principal Bundle|principal bundle]] of $G_{\mathrm{gauge}}$. The dynamic is governed by the [[Action (Physics)|action]] as [[Functional|functional]] $S[\phi,A]$. Using [[Principle of Least Action|the principle of least action]], *equations of motion (EOM)* can be derived.

To quantize the theory, one can employ two equivalent methods: [[Canonical Quantization|canonical method]] and [[Path Integral Quantization|path integral method]].
