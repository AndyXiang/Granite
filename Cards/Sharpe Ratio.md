The **Sharpe ratio**, also know as **reward-to-variability ratio**, measures the return per unit of risk for a risky asset compared to a risk-free asset. The formula is
$$S=\frac{E(r_{P})-r_{F}}{\sigma_{P}}$$
where $P$ is the risky asset, and $F$ is the risk-free asset.
