Let $M$ be a [[Complex Manifold|complex manifold]] with [[Almost Complex Manifold|almost complex structure]] $J$. Let $g$ be a [[Riemannian Metric|Riemannian metric]] over $M$ as a [[Differential Manifold|differential manifold]]. $g$ is called a **Hermitian metric** iff for every $X,Y\in \mathfrak{X}(M)$
$$g(J(X),J(Y))=g(X,Y).$$
Then $(M,J,g)$ is called a **Hermitian manifold**.

>[!note] Existence of Hermitian metric
>Any complex manifold admits a Hermitian metric.

## Properties
The Hermitian metric can be written as
$$g=g_{\mu \nu} \mathrm{d}z^{\mu}\otimes \mathrm{d}\bar{z}^{\nu}+\bar{g}_{\mu \nu} \mathrm{d}\bar{z}^{\mu}\otimes \mathrm{d}z^{\nu}$$
in [[Complex Manifold|holomorphic coordinates]].
