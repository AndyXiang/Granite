Let $X,Y$ be two [[Topological Space|topological spaces]]. $X,Y$ are of the same **homotopy type**, if there exists [[Continuous Map (Topology)|continuous maps]] $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to X$ s.t. $f\circ g$ is [[Homotopy|homotopic]] to $\mathrm{id}_{Y}$ and $g\circ f$ is homotopic to $\mathrm{id}_{X}$. These two maps are called the **homotopy equivalences**.

## Properties
1. If $X$ and $Y$ are [[Homeomorphism|homeomorphic]], then $X,Y$ are of the same homotopy type.
2. "Of the same homotopy type" is an [[Equivalence Relation|equivalence relation]].
