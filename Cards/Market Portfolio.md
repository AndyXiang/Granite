A **market [[Portfolio|portfolio]]** is a bundle of risky assets composed of every asset on the market, each with weight equals to the ratio of its total value and total value of the market, i.e. $w_{i}=V_{i} /\sum_{i} V_{i}$, where $V_{i}$ refers to the value. In practice, the market portfolio is approximated by some board [[Index Fund|index fund]].
