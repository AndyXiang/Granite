[[Group]] with commuting multiplication is a **abelian group**.

> [!theorem] Fundamental Theorem for Finitely Generated Abelian Groups
> Let $G$ be a finitely generated abelian group. Then it's [[Homomorphism|isomorphic]] to the direct product
>  $$G\cong \mathbb{Z}_{p_{1}^{r_{1}}}\times\cdots \times \mathbb{Z}_{p_{n}^{r_{n}}}\times \mathbb{Z}\times\cdots \times \mathbb{Z}.$$
>  The direct product is unique up to a permutation of factors.
